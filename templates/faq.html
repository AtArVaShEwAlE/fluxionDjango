How Fluxion works, step by step

10 questions
How do I upload a dataset?

Go to Dashboard, drag your CSV or Excel file into the upload box (or click browse). Max file size is 16MB. Once uploaded you'll see a preview with row and column counts.

What file types are supported?

CSV and Excel (.xlsx, .xls), up to 16MB.

How do I create a chart?

After uploading, hit "Create Chart" on the dataset preview. Pick one of 11 chart types (bar, line, pie, scatter, horizontal bar, area, doughnut, radar, stacked bar, combo or histogram), choose your columns, give it a title, and hit Update Chart to preview. The Customise panel lets you change the title, legend, axis titles, grid, font size, colours and line smoothing. Save Chart stores it under My Charts.

Can I edit a chart after saving it?

Yes, open it from My Charts and hit Edit to change the columns, chart type, colour scheme or any Customise option.

What's the difference between a Chart, a Report and a Board?

A Chart is one visualization. A Report bundles several saved charts behind a single shareable link, good for a weekly summary or client update. A Board puts saved charts in one grid where you pick each tile's size (small, medium or large) and order, and the board can refresh itself.

How do I build a board?

Open Boards, hit New Board, tick the saved charts you want, set a size for each tile, order them with the arrows and choose how often it refreshes (manual, every 30 seconds, every minute or every 5 minutes). On the board page you can refresh on demand and download the whole board as a PNG or PDF.

How do I share a chart, report or board?

Open it and hit Share. This generates a public link and anyone with the link can view it, no login needed. Hit the button again to copy the link. Hit Unshare and the link stops working straight away.

Can I embed a chart on my own site?

Yes. Charts and reports have an Embed button that copies an <iframe> snippet you can paste into any webpage. Boards are shared by link only.

What do people see when I share something?

Only the charts themselves, built from the summarised data each chart needs. Viewers never get your raw rows or the uploaded file, and they can't change anything.

What happens if I delete a chart that is in a report or board?

The report or board keeps working and simply skips the deleted chart. Deleting a report or board never deletes the charts inside it.
