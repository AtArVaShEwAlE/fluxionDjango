FAQ & Tutorials

How Fluxion works, step by step

How do I upload a dataset?

Go to Dashboard, drag your CSV or Excel file into the upload box (or click browse). Max file size is 16MB. Once uploaded you'll see a preview with row/column counts.

How do I create a chart?

After uploading, hit "Create Chart" on the dataset preview. Pick a chart type (bar, line, pie, scatter), choose your X/Y columns (or value/label for pie), give it a title, and hit Update Chart to preview. Save Chart stores it under My Charts.

What's the difference between a Chart and a Report?

A Chart is one visualization. A Report bundles several saved charts behind a single shareable link — good for a weekly summary or client update. Build one from the Reports page.

How do I share a chart or report?

Open the chart/report from My Charts or Reports, hit Share. This generates a public link — anyone with the link can view it, no login needed. Hit Share again anytime to copy the link.

Can I embed a chart on my own site?

Yes. Use the Embed button to copy an <iframe> snippet you can paste into any webpage.

Can I edit a chart after saving it?

Yes, open it from My Charts and hit Edit to change axis columns, chart type, or color scheme.

What file types are supported?

CSV and Excel (.xlsx, .xls), up to 16MB.
