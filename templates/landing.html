Why Fluxion

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Every feature below ships in the free plan — there's no upgrade wall between you and a finished chart.

Drag-and-drop upload

Drop a CSV or Excel file (up to 16MB) and Fluxion reads the columns, detects data types, and shows you the first rows immediately. No manual formatting, no cleaning spreadsheets before you can see your own data.

Four chart types

Bar, line, pie, and scatter plots cover most everyday reporting needs. Map any column to an axis, switch chart types without re-uploading, and watch the preview update instantly as you adjust it.

Save your work

Every chart is saved to your account alongside its source dataset, so you can revisit, tweak the axes, or export it again later without hunting for the original file or rebuilding from scratch.

Share with a link

Generate a public link to any saved chart in one click. Viewers get a clean, standalone page with the live chart and download options — no Fluxion account or login required on their end.

Export anywhere

Download any chart as a PNG for quick sharing or a PDF for print-ready reports. Exports render at full resolution, so they hold up fine dropped straight into a slide deck or document.

No setup time

There's no workspace to configure, no integration to connect, and no tutorial to sit through. Sign up, upload a file, and your first chart is ready in under a minute — genuinely.

The process

Three steps from spreadsheet to shareable chart

No account setup, no data modeling, no waiting on IT. Most people finish all three steps on their first visit.

01

Upload your file

Drop in a CSV or Excel file up to 16MB. Fluxion parses the columns, infers data types, and shows a live preview of your rows within seconds — before you commit to any chart settings.

02

Choose your chart

Pick bar, line, pie, or scatter, then map your columns to the axes (or values and labels, for pie charts). Adjust the color scheme and title, and the preview updates instantly on the right.

03

Save and share

Save the chart to your account so it's there whenever you come back, then generate a public link anyone can open and view — no Fluxion account required on their end, ever.
