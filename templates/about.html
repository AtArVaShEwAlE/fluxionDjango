About Fluxion

Why we built a data viz tool that gets out of your way

Our Story

Fluxion started as a simple idea: turning a spreadsheet into a chart shouldn't take twenty clicks and a tutorial video. We wanted something fast enough for a quick look at your data, and polished enough to share with a client.

Simple

Upload a CSV or Excel file, pick your columns, get a chart. No setup, no config files.

Shareable

Every chart and report gets a public link. Nobody needs an account to view your work.

Yours

Your data stays yours. Datasets and charts are tied to your account only.
