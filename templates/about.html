Our story

Why we built Fluxion

Fluxion started as a simple idea: turning a spreadsheet into a chart shouldn't take twenty clicks and a tutorial video. We wanted something fast enough for a quick look at your data, and polished enough to share with a client.

Today you can go from a raw file to a shareable chart, report or live-refreshing board without writing a line of code.

From spreadsheet to shareable chart, without the twenty clicks.

What we believe

Built around four ideas

Simple

Upload a CSV or Excel file, pick your columns, get a chart. No setup, no config files.

Flexible

Eleven chart types, plus options for titles, colours, legends and axes, so it looks the way you want.

Shareable

Every chart, report and board gets a public link. Nobody needs an account to view your work.

Yours

Your data stays yours. Datasets and charts are tied to your account only.
